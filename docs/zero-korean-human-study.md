# Zero-Korean comprehension study protocol

Use only approved, non-confidential or internally cleared artifacts. Recruit
English readers who cannot read Korean and provide the K-Slide report without
the original Korean artifact. Ask structured questions about decision status,
timing, key numbers, risk, dependency, trend, owner, and next step. Compare
answers with bilingual-reviewed gold answers and record time to answer,
confidence, review requests, and critical misunderstandings.

Do not report a comprehension score as translation accuracy. The study measures
whether the output removes the Korea-to-US understanding bottleneck.
